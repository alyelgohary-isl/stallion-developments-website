Superior execution, not excessive risk.

We believe strong risk-adjusted returns come from how well a project is built, not from how much leverage it carries. Every opportunity is tested against four questions before it reaches an investor: Is the market real? Is the approval path clear? Can we build it on budget? Is there a proven exit?

Our mission is to open access to institutional-quality real estate investing, with consistent performance driven by disciplined strategy and execution. Our vision is to become Ontario's most trusted development partner, known for transparency, performance and a commitment to investor success.

How we protect capital

Built-in protections

01

First-mortgage position.

Investor capital is secured by a registered first mortgage on the project land and improvements.

02

Conservative leverage.

We target a maximum 60% loan-to-value, which leaves a meaningful equity cushion beneath investor capital.

03

Partners invest first.

Stallion's partners put their own capital in ahead of investors. On Maple Place, that's $1.2M.

04

Permits in hand.

We raise construction capital only once land is owned and permits are secured.

05

Fixed-price construction.

Fixed-price subcontracts and a built-in contingency help contain cost overruns, and timeline buffers absorb Ontario winters.

06

Pre-sales de-risk the exit.

Pre-construction sales begin early, reducing exposure to market timing.

07

Independent appraisal.

Pricing is supported by a certified third-party appraisal and recent comparable sales.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of investment is this?

Current Stallion offerings are private debt, a loan to the project secured by a registered first mortgage on the property. Investors earn a fixed interest rate over a defined term.

Is the return guaranteed?

No. All real estate investments carry risk, including possible loss of capital. The first-mortgage position and conservative loan-to-value are designed to reduce risk, not eliminate it.

What is the minimum investment?

The minimum for Maple Place is $100,000.

How often will I hear from you?

Investors receive monthly reports with construction progress, budget tracking and milestone updates.

Can I exit early?

Private real estate debt is illiquid, so plan to hold for the full term. Early exits are not guaranteed.
