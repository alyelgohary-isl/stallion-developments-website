Why Muskoka

The Heart of Muskoka

Bracebridge, known as the Heart of Muskoka, is the seat of the District government. It has 17,305 permanent residents and a growing year-round community. It's also about 2 hours 15 minutes north of Toronto.

Why Prince Edward County

Wine and beach country

It's Ontario's wine and beach country, where limited supply meets growing demand for estate-scale living.

Why Waterloo Region

A tech and education hub

It's one of Canada's leading tech and education hubs, with steady demand for well-built detached family homes.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Stallion Developments build?

Stallion Developments builds premium new homes in Ontario. Current communities are in Bracebridge (Muskoka), Kitchener (Waterloo Region) and Prince Edward County.

Is Stallion Developments a Tarion-registered builder?

Yes. Stallion's CEO has been a Tarion-registered builder for more than 10 years, and new homes are covered by the Tarion new home warranty program.

Are there new homes for sale in Bracebridge right now?

Yes. Maple Place is a boutique collection of four semi-detached homes on Maple Street in Bracebridge. Each has about 1,900 sq ft, 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms. Register to receive floor plans and pricing.

Can I invest in Stallion's developments?

Accredited investors can participate in select projects through private debt secured by a registered first mortgage. Visit Invest With Us to book a confidential consultation.
