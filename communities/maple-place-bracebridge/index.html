Built for every season. Full-time living as well as weekends.

Muskoka, on your schedule.

Some people come to Muskoka for the weekend. Maple Place is for people who never want to leave. Set on quiet, established Maple Street in Bracebridge, it's a boutique collection of semi-detached homes, with limited stock available. Each is designed for both sides of Muskoka life: slow mornings on a private deck and a five-minute drive to the water, with coffee, groceries and the falls just around the corner.

Inside, open-concept living spaces fill with natural light, and premium finishes carry through every room. Outside, deep 108-foot lots give you room for a garden, a firepit, even a pool. It's cottage country without the cottage upkeep, and a year-round home that feels like a getaway.

Why Bracebridge

Why Bracebridge

A year-round town, not just a summer stop.

Bracebridge has 17,305 permanent residents, and it's the seat of the District of Muskoka's government.

Growth that's planned.

Muskoka's growth strategy anticipates 16,150 new permanent housing units between 2021 and 2051.

Limited new supply near downtown.

New homes this close to Muskoka's Downtown are rare, and it shows in the pricing: homes near Maple Street have sold at a premium to the town average.
