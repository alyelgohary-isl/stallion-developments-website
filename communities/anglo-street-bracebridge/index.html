Why Bracebridge

The Heart of Muskoka

Bracebridge, known as the Heart of Muskoka, is the seat of the District government. It has 17,305 permanent residents and a growing year-round community, and Muskoka's growth strategy anticipates 16,150 new permanent housing units between 2021 and 2051. It's about 2 hours 15 minutes north of Toronto.
